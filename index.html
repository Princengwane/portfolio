About Me

Hi, I'm Prince, a Software Engineer and AI Solutions Engineer focused on building technology that solves real business problems. I work across software development, AI, data, APIs, databases, and cloud technologies, taking ownership of solutions from understanding business requirements through to design, development, implementation, and ongoing improvement. I enjoy working directly with clients and stakeholders to understand their needs, translate business requirements into practical technical solutions, and deliver software that is reliable, scalable, and maintainable. My experience includes enterprise software development, AI-powered analytics, system integrations, database solutions, and on-premise AI platforms. Beyond writing code, I’m passionate about understanding why a solution is needed and how technology can improve business processes. I enjoy analysing problems, designing solutions, collaborating with people, and continuously learning new technologies to create meaningful and impactful software. I believe great software is not just about writing good code, it is about understanding the problem, taking ownership of the solution, and delivering value to the people who use it.
